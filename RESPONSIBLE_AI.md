# Responsible AI Use

AI assists. QGIS verifies. People decide.

1. Do not place confidential, personal, sensitive, or restricted LGU information in a public AI service.
2. Treat AI output as a draft. Check every field, expression, spatial operation, threshold, number, location, source, and conclusion.
3. Run spatial calculations in QGIS and keep the resulting project, processing history, and source references.
4. Separate verified observations from interpretation, limitations, and possible actions.
5. An authorized official or responsible professional makes the government decision.
