# Synthetic Training Data Notice

Every dataset in this repository is synthetic and created for classroom use. Names, locations, values, geometries, facilities, hazard boundaries, and analysis results do not represent official Lipa City records.

Do not use these files for operational planning, emergency response, regulatory review, taxation, engineering design, public advisories, or any official decision. Replace them with approved data from the responsible LGU office before adapting a workflow for real work.
