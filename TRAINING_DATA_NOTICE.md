# Training Data Notice

This repository contains a mix of official reference fields and synthetic training data.

For the ACT01 barangay profile, barangay names, PSGC codes, urban/rural classification, and 2024 population values are based on the Philippine Statistics Authority PSGC City of Lipa listing. All fields ending in `_synth` are synthetic training values created for classroom exploration. The ACT01 facility records, facility coordinates, and service-request records are also synthetic.

The reusable six-barangay QGIS project in `data/processed/lipa_training.gpkg`, including its facilities, roads, hazard polygons, geometries, values, and analysis outputs, remains synthetic.

Synthetic values and geometries do not represent official Lipa City operational records. Do not use them for operational planning, emergency response, regulatory review, taxation, engineering design, public advisories, or any official decision. Replace synthetic fields with approved data from the responsible LGU office before adapting a workflow for real work.

PSA reference for the ACT01 barangay reference fields: https://psa.gov.ph/classification/psgc/barangays/0401014000
